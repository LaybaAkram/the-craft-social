Home services

Some of what we teach, we also do at your home. Bridal henna, stitching pickups and event makeup, all within Karachi.

Bridal & event henna

A senior henna artist comes to your home for a bridal or family event. We arrive with our own cones, sealant, aftercare kit and design book. Timings and design scope are confirmed by phone before the visit.

  • From PKR 8,000 for a bridal booking
  • Two to four hours on site, depending on coverage
  • Available across Karachi, travel included within Garden West and Gulshan

Stitching pickups & alterations

Small tailoring jobs picked up from your home and returned finished within a week. Hems, minor repairs, taking in or letting out, and simple alterations to existing garments.

  • From PKR 1,500 per garment
  • Pickup and drop within Garden West and Gulshan
  • Priced per job after we see the garment

Event & party makeup

A makeup artist comes to your home for a party, engagement, shoot or mehndi. We bring the full kit — brushes, hygiene supplies, lashes and setting sprays. Hairstyling is available on request.

  • From PKR 6,500 per person
  • Roughly 90 minutes per look
  • Group bookings available for family events

Good to know

Travel

Travel is included within Garden West, Gulshan, North Nazimabad and Federal B Area. A small travel charge applies for DHA, Clifton, and areas beyond these.

Payment

Half is paid in advance to hold the booking, and the rest is settled on the day of the visit. We accept bank transfer, Easypaisa and cash.

Cancellations

Let us know at least 48 hours before the visit and the advance is returned in full. Less than 48 hours and the advance is kept.
