Why here

Affordable.

Monthly fees start at PKR 3,500. Every course includes the cones, fabric, thread and makeup kit you'll actually use: nothing to buy on your own.

Flexible.

Come to the studio, or join the same batch live on Zoom with the practice kit shipped to your address. Same mentors either way.

Open.

No prior experience needed. Most people who walk in have never held a henna cone or threaded a machine before. The first class assumes nothing.

A community-first studio.

The Craft Social started because learning a craft shouldn't require a big upfront investment or a formal art-school background. We keep groups small so mentors can sit with you at the table, and we keep the door open between batches so past students can keep using the machines and the design library.
