Courses

Three crafts, taught in small groups. Every course runs on a rolling schedule, new batches start every two weeks.

Show me this mode:

Intricate Henna Design

Traditional bridal patterns and the minimalist line-work that's more in demand now. You'll learn cone control, how to build a design from the wrist outward, and how to look after the stain so it lasts.

Four weeks Twice a week In-person studio practice

Contemporary Stitching

Start with seams and a straight stitch, work up to a finished garment. Machine and hand work, how to read a pattern, and the finishing details — hems, closures, pressing — that separate a home job from a wearable piece.

Six weeks Twice a week In-person studio practice

Editorial Makeup

Colour theory, contouring, and how to work for a camera rather than a mirror. Aimed at people who want to build a portfolio or work on shoots. Ends with a portrait session so you leave with images.

Five weeks Twice a week In-person studio practice

Materials

What's included in your course fee

Every item below is provided by the studio. You don't need to buy anything before your first class, and you keep whatever practice supplies are left at the end of the course.

Henna

  • Henna cones (natural paste, three per student)
  • Practice sheets and tracing paper
  • Design library access (printed and digital)
  • Sealant spray and aftercare balm
  • Cotton wraps and cellophane tape

Stitching

  • Practice fabric (two metres of cotton and lining)
  • Thread set, hand needles and machine bobbins
  • Tailor's chalk, measuring tape and pins
  • Paper patterns for two beginner garments
  • Machine access during studio hours

Editorial Makeup

  • Professional makeup kit (shared at the table)
  • Personal brush set to keep
  • Disposable applicators and hygiene supplies
  • Setting sprays and translucent powder
  • Portfolio photoshoot at the end of the course

Online students receive the same items as a shipped kit. The kit arrives within three to five working days of your first session.
